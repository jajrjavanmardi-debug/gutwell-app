4.6 Optional Context Inputs

You may optionally provide your current physical state (such as bloating, stomach pain, low energy, or nausea) and your planned activity after eating (such as working, studying, driving, travelling, exercising, attending a social event, managing a stressful day, or resting).

These inputs are:

  • Entirely voluntary — not required to use the app
  • Transmitted to Google's Gemini API as part of the meal analysis request
  • Used within that request to personalise the guidance returned
  • Not stored as a persistent profile field in GutWell's database
  • Not used to build a behavioural profile for advertising or any other purpose

Legal basis: Explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR) where current physical state is included; Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR otherwise.

5. Context-Aware Guidance

GutWell can optionally adapt meal guidance to your real-life situation. When you provide information about your current state or planned activities — such as working, studying, driving, travelling, exercising, attending a social event, managing a stressful day, or preparing for sleep — this information is included in the analysis request sent to Google's Gemini API to make the guidance more relevant.

This guidance uses cautious, non-prescriptive language such as "may feel more comfortable" and does not make guarantees. Context inputs are not stored as a permanent profile in GutWell's database. See Section 4.6 for the legal basis.
