How GutWell AI works

Three simple habits.
One growing picture.

The more consistently you log, the more useful your history becomes.

01

Check in daily — takes 60 seconds

Log how you feel each day — energy, mood, symptoms, and more. GutWell AI asks the right questions and stores everything so you don't have to.

02

Analyse a meal — by photo or text

Photograph what you eat, or simply describe it in your own words. GutWell AI notes what you ate, how you felt at the time, and what you were planning to do next, then gives you a Meal Impact Score. If it got something wrong, Refine Analysis lets you correct it. Photo analysis requires GutWell Premium; describing a meal in text does not.

03

Review your patterns over time

Your GutWell Score updates daily based on your check-ins. Over time, your history gives you something to reflect on — bringing possible patterns and your Well-Tolerated Foods into view.

What GutWell AI includes

Built around your daily life,
not a generic gut.

📊

Your GutWell Score

A daily summary number calculated from your real check-in data — stool type, symptoms, energy, and mood. It's a reflection tool, not a medical measurement. It becomes more useful as you log consistently.

📷

Meal Analysis — photo or text

Scan a meal, or describe it in words, and add brief context — how you feel and what you're doing next. You get a Meal Impact Score plus “What GutWell considered”, so the reasoning behind it is visible rather than hidden. Photo analysis requires GutWell Premium.

📈

Personal Pattern Review

After consistent check-ins, your history starts to show possible patterns — foods that appear alongside better or worse days, and a Well-Tolerated Foods list built from your own logs. GutWell AI surfaces these for you to review and reflect on, never as a verdict.

🎯

Contextual Guidance

Meal suggestions consider your gut profile, current symptoms, and what you're doing after eating — whether that's working, exercising, driving, or resting. Practical guidance for real life.

Your data, your questions

Challenge the analysis.
Ask your own questions.

GutWell AI gives you an analysis — but you are not required to accept it. If the AI misidentified your meal, got the context wrong, or gave advice that doesn't feel right, you can push back directly.

Refine Analysis lets you correct the meal and add what you actually ate. Ask what a different choice might have looked like. GutWell AI revises its guidance based on what you tell it — not the other way around.

You are talking to your own health data. GutWell AI is the interface — not the authority.

Refine Analysis · example

This looks like fried fish with a creamy dip — Meal Impact Score 5/10 for today.
That was grilled, not fried. And I had it with yogurt, not dip.
Thanks for the correction. Grilled fish with yogurt is lighter — I'd revise the Meal Impact Score to 7/10. The yogurt is worth watching if you're sensitive to dairy.
What if I had skipped the yogurt entirely?
Without the dairy, this would be a straightforward high-protein meal — likely a Meal Impact Score of 8/10 today.

Guidance for real life

GutWell AI adapts to what you're doing next.

Most nutrition apps give the same advice regardless of your situation. GutWell AI considers your real-life context — not just what's on your plate.

Heading to a job interview? Driving for two hours? Going to sleep soon? About to exercise? The guidance you receive adapts to your situation, using careful language that reflects possibility rather than certainty.

GutWell AI does not make guarantees. It offers practical, situation-aware suggestions — the kind a knowledgeable friend might offer, not a clinical prescription.
