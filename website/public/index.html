The problem

Gut health is personal.
Generic advice rarely helps.

Most people dealing with digestive discomfort already know something is off. What they lack is a clear picture of their own patterns.

🫧

You notice symptoms — but not the cause

Bloating, discomfort, energy crashes after meals. It's hard to know whether it's the food, the timing, the stress, or something else entirely.

📋

Tracking feels like a chore

Notebooks and spreadsheets don't connect the dots. Most apps only count calories — they don't consider how you felt, what you did, or what happened next.

🔍

You want your own answers

Not averages, not generic elimination diets. You want to understand what seems to work for your body specifically — and be able to review it over time.

How GutWell works

Three simple habits.
One growing picture.

The more consistently you log, the more useful your history becomes.

01

Check in daily — takes 60 seconds

Log how you feel each day: stool type, bloating, pain, energy level, and mood. GutWell asks the right questions and remembers everything so you don't have to.

02

Scan your meals (optional)

Photograph what you eat and describe your situation. GutWell notes what you ate, how you felt at the time, and what you were planning to do next.

03

Review your patterns over time

Your Gut Score updates daily based on your check-ins. Over time, your history gives you something to reflect on — and brings your personal patterns into view.

What GutWell includes

Built around your daily life,
not a generic gut.

📊

Your Gut Score

A daily summary number calculated from your real check-in data — stool type, symptoms, energy, and mood. It's a reflection tool, not a medical measurement. It becomes more useful as you log consistently.

📷

Meal Photo Analysis

Scan a meal and add brief context — how you feel and what you're doing next. GutWell considers the whole picture, not just the food, and gives you a practical, non-judgmental perspective.

📈

Personal Pattern Review

After consistent check-ins, your history starts to reveal possible patterns — foods that appear alongside better or worse days. GutWell surfaces these for you to review and reflect on.

🎯

Contextual Guidance

Meal suggestions consider your gut profile, current symptoms, and what you're doing after eating — whether that's working, exercising, driving, or resting. Practical guidance for real life.
